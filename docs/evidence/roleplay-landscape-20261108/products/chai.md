# CHAI

Reviewed 2026-11-08. CHAI is Chai Research Corp.'s social AI character platform for adult entertainment and roleplay. The supplied [research homepage](https://www.chai-research.com/) links to [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Beauchamp.Messenger.external&hl=en_US), whose developer identity and website link agree. This reciprocal link supports official identity rather than relying on the supplied domain alone.

This review covers public product descriptions, policy and a company-linked community announcement. No account, chat generation, purchase, device test or interactive UI inspection occurred. Benefits below are design inferences, not measured outcomes. The [Chai AI web FAQ](https://www.chai-ai.com/faq) identifies the same operator and links its privacy policy, but I found no reciprocal link to that web domain on the research homepage. Treat its claims as attributed web documentation, not proof of app behavior.

## Supported strengths

- A character catalog supplies an immediate starting point. Google Play describes selecting characters, while the web FAQ describes community rankings and character creation. Selection can replace the blank-page task of inventing a companion. Neither source establishes card import, search quality or the details shown before starting a story. [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Beauchamp.Messenger.external&hl=en_US), [FAQ](https://www.chai-ai.com/faq).
- Creation has a publication boundary. CHAI documents keeping a new bot private or making it available to others. That distinction can let someone develop a premise before sharing it. The creation form, defaults and ability to preview the opening were not established. [Privacy policy](https://chai-research.com/privacy/).
- Memories and personas have explicit saved state. The policy names both. This suggests a place for persistent story facts and the user's role, rather than repeatedly inserting them into dialogue. Actual retrieval, scope and capacity remain unknown, and current availability needs qualification below. [Privacy policy](https://chai-research.com/privacy/).
- Discovery and conversation have separate safety actions. CHAI documents anonymous character/message reporting, search filtering, hiding explicit messages and blocking characters. These mechanisms can help users remove an unwanted encounter without abandoning the whole catalog. Their effectiveness was not tested. [Safety Center](https://www.chai-research.com/safety.html).

## Tradeoffs and unknowns

Continuity is the largest evidence gap. A [founder-signed announcement](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChaiApp/comments/1xnb7a6/persona_memory_update/) in the subreddit linked by the research homepage reports a Persona & Memory technical problem. Restoration was expected within 24 to 48 hours. The author account also appears as a brand affiliate in that subreddit. The retrieved timestamp is relative and inconsistent with crawl metadata. No restoration confirmation was established, so neither permanent removal nor recovery is proven.

The web FAQ explicitly says Web Pro and the app are separate platforms with no data sharing. Do not promise that switching clients continues the same story. It also documents mobile-browser access. Android distribution is established by Google Play; the linked Apple listing returned a fetch 404, which does not prove delisting. No mobile ergonomics were tested. [FAQ](https://www.chai-ai.com/faq), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Beauchamp.Messenger.external&hl=en_US).

The policy names image/video generation and web voice, but does not establish provider selection or voice configuration. It describes conversation use for model improvement after identifier removal. Its EU/UK portability request is not evidence of an in-product chat export. [Privacy policy](https://chai-research.com/privacy/). The FAQ's blanket no-third-party-sharing wording conflicts with the policy's supplier disclosures. Do not repeat that privacy assurance without qualification. [FAQ](https://www.chai-ai.com/faq).

Exact-conversation selection, archives, edit/retry/branch, context inspection, lorebooks, external model connections, sampler controls, group roleplay and deployable self-hosting remain unknown. Vendor infrastructure claims do not establish user model control. Public material also does not establish failed-send recovery or backup restoration. Unknown means not established, not absent.

CHAI's adults-only rule does not imply unrestricted generation. Its Safety Center describes restricted-content refusals and paid access in some regions. Google Play showed Mature 17+, despite the Safety Center's claim that both stores rate it 18+. The service age rule and store rating should not be conflated. [Safety Center](https://www.chai-research.com/safety.html), [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Beauchamp.Messenger.external&hl=en_US).

## Proposals for the fork

- Put premise and opening beside the character's start action. Catalog selection should help someone choose a story.
- Make private versus shared creation explicit before publication.
- Keep persona and story-memory controls identifiable, with scope visible. Do not claim CHAI proves recall quality.
- Show continuity failures where users select or resume a story, including what remains saved and available recovery actions.

Do not copy platform separation that surprises users expecting the same conversation. Avoid blanket privacy copy, implied unlimited availability, or discovery that obscures the exact saved story. These are proposals, not implementation commitments or claims that the fork lacks native controls.
