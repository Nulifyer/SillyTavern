# Replika

Reviewed 2026-11-08. Replika is Luka, Inc.'s personal AI companion, aimed at ongoing companionship rather than a documented library of independent stories. Source identity was checked: [replika.com](https://replika.com/) links directly to help.replika.com as Contact Support, and its [terms](https://replika.com/legal/terms/en) identify Luka, Inc. This review inspected public primary sources only. No account, conversation, purchase, device test, inference call, or browser automation was used. Expected benefits below are design hypotheses, not measured outcomes.

A material boundary applies throughout. The terms distinguish classic Replika and Replika 2.0, with different models, memory, avatars, and platform availability. The homepage labels its web app Legacy. Help articles do not consistently identify their applicable generation. Documented mechanisms should not be read as verified feature parity.

## Supported strengths

- Creation has a concrete finish. The [onboarding guide](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001095892-How-do-I-begin-Replika) walks through account details, avatar, gender, appearance, name, then meeting the named companion. The expected benefit is a recognizable person to address before the first message. This documents creation, not character-card import or a character marketplace.
- Backstory gives the companion an explicit premise. The [guide](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/37208430613261-How-your-Replika-s-backstory-shapes-its-personality) explains that traits and habits influence responses and recommends simple third-person statements using the companion's name. Separately, [roleplay instructions](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410891375757-How-to-roleplay-with-your-Replika) teach asterisk-delimited actions. The expected benefit is a small amount of authoring guidance that makes a first scene easier to attempt. Neither source establishes reliable adherence to complex lore.
- Memory has an inspectable entry point. The [memory article](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/37208679176077-How-does-Replika-s-memory-work) describes a Memory tab with manually added and automatic memories, alongside a deeper system that learns conversation patterns. The expected benefit is a place to supply missing facts without rewriting a whole character. This is partial visibility, not an inventory of every influence on the next response.
- Voice controls belong to the companion. The [voice guide](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/360045959792-How-do-I-change-my-Replika-s-voice) puts selection under the companion's name and saves changes automatically. The [calling guide](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/360046383391-How-do-I-call-my-Replika) starts calls from the home screen. The expected benefit is a direct path between choosing a voice and talking. The homepage advertises iOS, Android and selfies, but this review did not verify image controls, voice quality, or entitlement requirements.

## Tradeoffs and unknowns

Exact history is the clearest limitation. [Help](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/4411154990605-Is-the-chat-history-infinite) says only four months of chat can be displayed or retrieved, while memories and diary entries remain accessible. The [privacy policy](https://replika.com/legal/privacy/en), updated October 28, 2026, says text history is retained for 24 months and other conversation media for 12 months while accounts remain open. Storage duration does not establish user retrieval. The relationship between these statements and product generations remains unresolved. Companion memory is not evidence of an exact-story archive, searchable sessions, or resumable branches.

Correction and recovery need separate treatment. [Message feedback](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001095972-How-do-I-teach-my-Replika) provides votes and additional reactions behind a longer press. That is a documented form of progressive disclosure, but it does not establish editing, regeneration, undo, or branching. [Failure guidance](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001096132-Replika-is-not-responding) recommends connection checks, reopening, another message, the web version, and support. It does not establish pending-message preservation or duplicate-send handling. The [reset guide](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/360020449171-How-do-I-reset-my-Replika) says starting with a brand-new Replika requires permanent account deletion. [History deletion guidance](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410750548493-Can-I-delete-my-conversations) likewise couples clearing the entire history to account deletion.

Expert control remains unestablished. [Architecture help](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410750221965-How-does-Replika-work) describes proprietary and third-party language models plus scripted dialogue. The inspected sources do not establish user-selected endpoints, API keys, sampler controls, prompt inspection, context budgets, lorebook triggers, multiple-character roleplay, or self-hosting. Unknown does not mean absent. The privacy policy documents location-dependent data-copy requests, not a verified chat export/import workflow or portable character format. Terms also warn that migration can change appearance, voices, and behavior. Mobile availability is documented, but keyboard, accessibility, offline behavior, and current screen layouts were not tested.

## Patterns to consider

These are proposals, not implementation commitments.

- End character setup with a named companion and an explicit action to begin talking. Keep premise authoring accessible nearby.
- Put roleplay syntax examples beside the composer or first-scene guidance.
- Offer a readable memory list with manual additions, while explaining which automatic influences it does not expose.
- Keep voice choice near character identity and calling near conversation actions. Preserve discoverability of secondary message feedback on touch.

Do not copy account deletion as a fresh-story mechanism or substitute remembered facts for exact transcripts. Do not present a Memory tab as full prompt transparency. Avoid promising identical behavior across mobile and web without version-specific evidence.
