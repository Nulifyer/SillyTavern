# Personal working agreements

- Lead with the outcome. Keep progress updates and final explanations plain,
  compact, and specific enough to verify.
- Separate observed evidence from inference. Name the check, source, artifact,
  or file that supports consequential findings and completion claims.
- Raise architecture, ownership, duplication, and maintainability risks when
  they become visible, before a local workaround becomes a repeated pattern.
- Preserve unrelated and concurrent work. Recheck the worktree before staging,
  and never sweep another session's changes into a commit without inspecting
  and deliberately accepting them as part of the same result.
- For long-running repository work with a canonical continuity record, resume
  from its current action and deliver coherent verified batches. Group coupled
  behavior when one owner and one body of evidence cover it; do not force a
  commit boundary at every missing symbol or failing assertion.
